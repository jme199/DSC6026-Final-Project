\author{DSC6026 Final Project Team\\Member B}

\begin{abstract}
We compare Louvain communities and degree-corrected stochastic block model
(SBM) groups on the combined SNAP Facebook ego network. Louvain found 16
communities with modularity 0.835, while the minimum-description-length SBM
fit found 60 structural blocks. The adjusted Rand index between the two
partitions was 0.374. The top 20 nodes by betweenness had larger mean
fractions of incident edges crossing communities and blocks than the top 20
by PageRank. The rankings overlapped on 10 nodes and had nearly equal mean
counts of edges crossing SBM blocks. These comparisons characterize where
the two sets sit in the network used for the team's diffusion experiments.
\end{abstract}
